    How to play

    Put your finger on the ball, drag back to set power, and let go. Pull straight back for a straight shot; lean right and it hooks, lean left and it slices. Drag anywhere else to aim, or tap the map. Pick clubs along the bottom, shape the shot with ◀ ▶ and flight it with ▼ ▲. On the green, light and dark shading shows the high and low side, and thin lines drift the way it breaks.

    The real course: holes, bunkers, greens and water traced by © OpenStreetMap contributors, on USGS 3DEP 1-metre elevation. Ball flight calibrated to TrackMan tour averages. Sounds: Freesound recordings (CC0) by Scottrex05, Mikes-MultiMedia, lmbubec, zolopher and aidansamuel. Unofficial fan game; not affiliated with the courses.
