    How to play

    Put your finger on the ball, drag back to set power, and let go. Pull straight back for a straight shot; lean right and it hooks, lean left and it slices. Drag anywhere else to aim, or tap the map. Pick clubs along the bottom, shape the shot with ◀ ▶ and flight it with ▼ ▲. On the green, the arrows show the break.

    The real course: holes, bunkers, greens and water traced by © OpenStreetMap contributors, on USGS 3DEP 1-metre elevation. Ball flight calibrated to TrackMan tour averages. Unofficial fan game; not affiliated with the course.
